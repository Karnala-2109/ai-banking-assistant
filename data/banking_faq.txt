BANKING FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Minimum Balance

The minimum balance requirement depends on the type of bank account and the applicable account policy. Customers should check the specific requirements for their account type.

What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is a fixed scheduled payment that a borrower makes toward a loan. An EMI generally consists of both principal and interest components.

How is EMI calculated?

The EMI depends on the loan principal, interest rate, and repayment tenure. A higher loan amount or interest rate generally results in a higher EMI, while a longer tenure may reduce the monthly EMI but can increase the total interest paid.

What happens if I miss an EMI?

Missing or delaying an EMI may result in additional charges according to the applicable loan terms. Repeated missed payments may negatively affect the customer's credit history and could affect future borrowing opportunities.

How can I check my loan status?

Customers can check their loan status through the bank's official digital banking channels or by contacting customer support.

What is a credit score?

A credit score is a numerical indicator used by lenders to assess a customer's creditworthiness. A stronger credit history may improve the chances of obtaining credit on favorable terms.

What is KYC?

KYC stands for Know Your Customer. It is a process used by banks to verify the identity and address of customers.

Why is KYC required?

Banks use KYC procedures to verify customer identity and comply with applicable banking and regulatory requirements.

Can I apply for a loan online?

Depending on the product and bank policy, customers may be able to initiate or complete a loan application through the bank's official digital channels.

How can I contact customer support?

Customers should use the official customer-support channels provided by their bank for assistance with account or banking-related issues.
