HOME LOAN POLICY

Home Loan Eligibility

Applicants must generally be at least 21 years old and have a stable source of income. Eligibility depends on income, age, employment status, credit history, existing financial obligations, and the property's value.

Required Documents

The following documents may be required when applying for a home loan:

1. Identity proof such as Aadhaar Card, Passport, PAN Card, or Driving License.
2. Address proof.
3. Recent passport-size photographs.
4. Salary slips for the required recent months for salaried applicants.
5. Bank statements.
6. Income tax returns or income documents where applicable.
7. Employment or business proof.
8. Property-related documents.
9. PAN card and other KYC documents.

Home Loan Interest Rate

The applicable interest rate depends on the applicant's credit profile, loan amount, tenure, income, and prevailing bank policy. Customers should check the current applicable rate before applying.

Home Loan Tenure

Home loans may be offered for different repayment periods depending on the applicant's age, income, loan amount, and bank policy.

Processing

The bank verifies the applicant's identity, income, credit history, repayment capacity, and property documents before making a final lending decision.
