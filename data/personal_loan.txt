PERSONAL LOAN POLICY

Personal Loan Overview

A personal loan is an unsecured loan that can be used for personal financial needs such as education, medical expenses, travel, home renovation, or other eligible purposes.

Personal Loan Eligibility

Applicants must generally meet the bank's minimum age and income requirements. Eligibility may depend on income, employment status, credit history, existing loans, monthly obligations, and repayment capacity.

Required Documents

Applicants may be asked to provide:

1. Identity proof such as Aadhaar Card, Passport, PAN Card, or Driving License.
2. Address proof.
3. Recent passport-size photographs.
4. Salary slips or income proof.
5. Recent bank statements.
6. Employment proof for salaried applicants.
7. Income tax documents where applicable.
8. PAN card and other KYC documents.

Personal Loan Interest Rate

The interest rate depends on factors such as the applicant's credit score, income, employment profile, loan amount, repayment tenure, and prevailing bank policy.

Personal Loan Tenure

The repayment tenure depends on the loan amount, applicant profile, and bank policy. Customers should confirm the available tenure at the time of application.

Security

Personal loans are generally unsecured, meaning collateral is normally not required. However, approval is subject to the bank's credit assessment.

Repayment

The borrower must repay the loan through scheduled monthly installments. Missing or delaying an EMI may result in additional charges and may negatively affect the borrower's credit history.
