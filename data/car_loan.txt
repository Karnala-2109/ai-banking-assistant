CAR LOAN POLICY

Car Loan Overview

A car loan is a financing facility that helps customers purchase a new or used vehicle. The loan amount and terms depend on the applicant's profile, vehicle value, and bank policy.

Car Loan Eligibility

Applicants must generally satisfy the bank's minimum age and income requirements. Eligibility may depend on income, employment status, credit history, existing financial commitments, repayment capacity, and the value of the vehicle.

Required Documents

Applicants may be asked to provide:

1. Identity proof such as Aadhaar Card, Passport, PAN Card, or Driving License.
2. Address proof.
3. Recent passport-size photographs.
4. Salary slips or income proof.
5. Recent bank statements.
6. Employment or business proof.
7. PAN card and other KYC documents.
8. Vehicle quotation or proforma invoice from the dealer.
9. Vehicle-related documents where applicable.

Car Loan Interest Rate

The applicable interest rate depends on factors such as the applicant's credit profile, income, employment status, loan amount, vehicle type, repayment tenure, and prevailing bank policy.

Car Loan Tenure

The repayment tenure depends on the applicant's profile, loan amount, vehicle type, and bank policy.

Down Payment

The customer may need to contribute a portion of the vehicle's purchase price as a down payment. The required amount depends on the vehicle, loan amount, and applicable bank policy.

Repayment

The borrower repays the loan through scheduled monthly installments. Missing or delaying an EMI may result in additional charges and may negatively affect the borrower's credit history.
