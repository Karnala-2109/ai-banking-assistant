DEBIT CARD POLICY

Debit Card Overview

A debit card allows customers to make purchases, withdraw cash from ATMs, and perform other eligible transactions using funds available in their bank account.

How to Block a Debit Card

If a debit card is lost, stolen, or suspected to be compromised, the customer should block the card immediately using the bank's official digital banking channel, customer support service, or other authorized card-blocking facility.

Customers should act quickly to prevent unauthorized transactions.

What Information May Be Required?

The customer may need to provide identifying information to verify their identity before the card is blocked.

After Blocking the Card

Once the card is blocked, customers should contact the bank through official channels to understand the process for obtaining a replacement card.

Unauthorized Transactions

Customers should report any suspected unauthorized debit-card transactions to the bank immediately through official customer-support channels.

Card Security

Customers should never share their PIN, OTP, CVV, passwords, or other confidential banking credentials with anyone.

Customers should regularly monitor their account transactions and report suspicious activity promptly.
